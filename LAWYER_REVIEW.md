# Lawyer review checklist

Please check each item against current law and official sources, and mark it OK, CHANGE or REMOVE.
Page: `index.html`. Hindi text is in the same file and also needs a native-speaker review.
New sections (notice and hearing, RTI) are English only, pending your sign-off, so they are translated only once.

## A. Constitution
1. Art. 19(1)(a), (b), (c) wording and plain-language summaries.
2. Art. 19(2) and 19(3): list of grounds for restriction, and the claim that restrictions must be reasonable and proportionate.
3. Art. 20(3), Art. 21, Art. 22(1) and (2) (grounds, lawyer of choice, 24 hours excluding travel).
4. Art. 32 and 226 (habeas corpus example).
5. Art. 324 to 326: Election Commission's role, no exclusion on religion, race, caste or sex, voting age 18.
6. Art. 51A(i): duty to safeguard public property and abjure violence.

## B. Arrest, detention, protest law
7. Section 163 BNSS as the successor to Section 144 CrPC, and the claim that orders must be reasoned and proportionate.
8. Detention checklist: ask the reason, ask for a lawyer, inform a family member, medical examination, note officers' details.
9. Wording on silence: "cannot be forced to make self-incriminating statements; ask for your lawyer; this does not mean you can refuse every question."
10. Women: arrest between sunset and sunrise, the exception (written report, prior permission of the Judicial Magistrate First Class), and search by a woman officer. Add the exact BNSS section numbers.
11. Permission for protests: written application to police, acknowledgement, and the statement that rules vary by city and state.
12. Delhi-specific statements (designated protest sites), if kept.
13. Loudspeaker rules and night-time restrictions.
14. Offence for disobeying a prohibitory order (confirm the BNS section) and the advice to comply and challenge later in court.
15. Advice on recording and posting images of police or of other protesters.
16. Is the "peaceful protest guide" safe to publish as written? Anything that could be read as encouraging unlawful assembly?

## D. Voter roll
24. Four qualifying dates (1 Jan, 1 Apr, 1 Jul, 1 Oct) and advance application from age 17.
25. Purpose of Forms 6, 7 and 8, and the document lists on the page.
26. Alternative photo IDs for voting and the wording "you must be on the roll".
27. Description of SIR (Special Intensive Revision) and the neutral tone about the dispute.
28. Appeal route: ERO, then District Magistrate, then Chief Electoral Officer, with the time limits (Registration of Electors Rules, 1960). Add the exact time limits.
29. Article 226 as a remedy for wrongful deletion.
30. Voter helpline 1950, and BLO verification visit.

## E. RTI and petitions
31. RTI Act, 2005: PIO, fee and BPL exemption, 30 days and 48 hours, first appeal 30 days, second appeal 90 days.
32. rtionline.gov.in covers central bodies only. Say so clearly if not already clear.
33. Sample RTI question: is it properly framed, and is the privacy caveat accurate?
34. CPGRAMS (pgportal.gov.in) for central grievances.
