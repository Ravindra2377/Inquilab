## Section H: Bilingual Parity & Native Hindi Legal Review
*Specialist domain: Legal Linguistics & Hindi Devanagari Translation*

All 51 provisions and UI labels have paired English and Hindi texts in `index.html`. Legal reviewers fluent in Hindi must verify that:
1. **Accurate Terminology**:
   - Fundamental Rights = मौलिक अधिकार
   - Reasonable Restrictions = युक्तियुक्त निर्बंधन
   - Self-Incrimination = आत्म-अभिशंसन / अपने विरुद्ध गवाही
   - Cognizable / Non-Cognizable = संज्ञेय / असंज्ञेय
   - Habeas Corpus = बंदी प्रत्यक्षीकरण
   - Ordinary Resident = साधारण निवासी
   - Electoral Roll = निर्वाचक नामावली
   - Special Intensive Revision = विशेष गहन पुनरीक्षण
   - First Appellate Authority = प्रथम अपीलीय प्राधिकारी
2. **Grammar & Tone**: Neutral, constitutional tone without colloquial distortion.
3. **Typography**: Ensure appropriate `lang="hi"` tags (such as hero slogan `<p class="slogan" lang="hi">इंकलाब ज़िंदाबाद</p>`).
